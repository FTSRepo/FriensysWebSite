## Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to all users of the Friensys School ERP platform — school administrators, staff, and any other authorised users. By using the platform, you agree to comply with this AUP in addition to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service).

## Permitted Uses

The Friensys platform may be used to:

- Manage student admissions, attendance, academic records, and examinations
- Process fee collections, generate invoices, and manage school finances
- Communicate with parents and students through authorised channels
- Generate reports for internal school administration and regulatory compliance
- Store and retrieve staff, payroll, and HR-related data for the school's own use

## Prohibited Activities

### Data Misuse
- Uploading data for purposes unrelated to school administration
- Accessing or attempting to access other schools' data
- Exporting bulk student data for third-party marketing or commercial resale
- Storing content that is defamatory, obscene, or in violation of any law

### Security Violations
- Attempting to penetrate, probe, or test the security of our systems without authorisation
- Introducing malware, viruses, ransomware, or any malicious code
- Using automated scripts or bots to access the platform without written consent
- Sharing login credentials or allowing unauthorised individuals to access the account

### Platform Misuse
- Reverse-engineering, decompiling, or disassembling any part of the platform
- Scraping or systematically downloading data to build competing products
- Impersonating Friensys staff or other schools' administrators
- Using the platform to send spam, phishing content, or unsolicited bulk messages

### Legal and Regulatory Violations
- Processing personal data in violation of DPDPA 2023 or other applicable laws
- Using the platform for any unlawful purpose under Indian or international law
- Facilitating fraud, money laundering, or financial crimes

## Enforcement

Violations of this AUP may result in:

1. **Warning**: First minor violation — written notice to the school administrator
2. **Temporary suspension**: Continued or serious violations — access suspended pending investigation
3. **Termination**: Deliberate, repeated, or severe violations — contract terminated per Section 11 of the Terms of Service
4. **Legal action**: Violations involving criminal activity or significant harm may be reported to law enforcement

We reserve the right to investigate suspected violations and cooperate with authorities as required by law.
