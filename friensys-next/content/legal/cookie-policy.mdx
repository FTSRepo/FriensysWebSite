## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, maintain your session, and understand how you use the site.

## Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the platform to function and cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `fs_session` | Maintains your logged-in session | Session |
| `fs_csrf` | Protects against cross-site request forgery | Session |
| `fs_theme` | Remembers your light/dark mode preference | 1 year |

### Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our website. These cookies collect anonymised usage data — pages visited, session duration, and referring sources. No personally identifiable information is sent to Google.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|----------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Google | Distinguishes users | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Google | Maintains session state | 2 years |

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your choices to improve your experience.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `fs_lang` | Stores language preference | 1 year |
| `fs_onboarding` | Tracks completion of onboarding steps | 30 days |

## Marketing Cookies

We do **not** use third-party advertising cookies or behavioural tracking cookies on the Friensys platform or website.

## Managing Cookies

### Browser Settings
Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies through their settings. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging into the platform.

- **Chrome**: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- **Firefox**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Edge**: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
- **Safari**: Preferences → Privacy

### Cookie Banner
On your first visit to friensys.com, you will see a cookie consent banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies.
